1. Product terminology to lock in

Moments

Viewing destination

Full-screen, vertical discovery for watching and interacting with Sparks. Think of Moments as Niakofa's viewing experience—not the name of each post.

Sparks

Individual content

The short-form videos, photos, responses, and creative posts people capture and publish. The creation action is Create a Spark.

Niakofa Family Stories / Griot Family Stories

Enduring family legacy

Family narratives, oral histories, elders' memories, genealogy, and preserved family milestones. These belong in the Family Vault / Legacy experience—not in the 24-hour short-form feed.

This aligns with the repository's product decision: “Create a Spark,” “Explore Moments,” and reserve Stories for longer-form narratives and Legacy for preserved cultural and family memory. View product decision in GitHub

My recommendations

Build Moments as a dedicated viewing destination, reusing existing authorized media and Story compatibility rather than replacing the backend.

Use one consistent Create Spark visual journey across Community and Spirals, but preserve the distinct Exchange listing upload lifecycle.

Give Family Stories a separate, private-by-default experience in Family Vault / Legacy, with oral histories, transcripts, family contributors, timeline, and preservation controls.

Complete the remaining server-backed discovery, moderation, remix rights, accessibility, analytics, and media cleanup loops before describing the full feature set as complete.

Keep Nia AI separate from the Niakofa media pipeline.
