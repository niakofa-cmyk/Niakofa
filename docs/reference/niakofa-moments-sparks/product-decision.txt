Yes — I agree with that distinction, and I would change the architecture accordingly.

The cleanest model is:

Niakofa term	What it represents	User action
SPARKS	The individual short-form posts/videos	Create a Spark
MOMENTS	The immersive short-form viewing experience/destination	Explore Moments
STORIES	Longer-form, enduring narratives	Share a Story
SPIRALS	Community groups/interest circles	Join/Create a Spiral
GRIOT / LEGACY	Preserved cultural, family, and historical memory	Preserve a Legacy

So, yes: users create Sparks, not Moments.

The key distinction

Think of it like this:

Instagram

Reel = individual video
Reels = viewing destination

YouTube

Short = individual video
Shorts = viewing destination

For Niakofa:

Spark = individual piece of content
Moments = the place where Sparks are experienced

So:

Create a Spark.
Discover Sparks in Moments.

That gives Niakofa a much stronger product vocabulary.

What I would change in the current Community

Instead of:

Home | Stories | Spirals | People | Notifications

I would use:

Home | Moments | Spirals | People | Notifications

Then inside Moments:

Moments

The Global Village in motion.

+ Create a Spark

And the content cards/items are explicitly called Sparks.

For example:

“Create a Spark”
“Post a Spark”
“Share this Spark”
“React to this Spark”
“Save this Spark”
“Your Sparks”
“Trending Sparks”
“Sparks from Fort Worth”
“Sparks from your Spirals”
And this resolves the Stories problem

I would not simply rename the current Stories tab to Sparks.

That would make the destination sound like a collection of individual posts rather than an experience.

Instead:

Stories → Moments

while:

Story → Spark only for the short-form content model.

Then reserve Stories for actual narrative content.

For example:

Community Stories — “The story of how a Fort Worth neighbor helped another family.”

versus:

Spark — a 30-second video showing that act of kindness.

And eventually:

Legacy — that story preserved as part of the community's history.

That gives you a very coherent hierarchy:

SPARK → MOMENT → STORY → LEGACY

Not every Spark becomes a Story, and not every Story becomes a Legacy.

One important change from our previous recommendation

Since you've officially decided on Spirals, I would now lock the primary Community architecture as:

HOME · MOMENTS · SPIRALS · PEOPLE · NOTIFICATIONS

with Messages remaining a persistent header action.

And I would make Moments the replacement for the current Stories destination, while Sparks becomes the fundamental short-form content object users actually create.

That's the terminology I would now carry into the GitHub implementation, documentation, routes, analytics, and UI copy.
