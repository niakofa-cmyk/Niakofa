# Niakofa Spirals canonical product

Niakofa Spirals is the public product surface. User-facing terminology is
Spiral, not Circle.

Circle-era routes, database tables, storage identifiers, and internal types
remain compatibility infrastructure. They must not be destructively renamed
just to remove legacy identifiers.

Spiral discovery shows active, verified neighborhood Spirals. The current
Map-Locator-matched neighborhood is promoted first, followed by other active
neighborhood Spirals. City-wide or unverified/generated neighborhoods are not
part of the discovery list.
