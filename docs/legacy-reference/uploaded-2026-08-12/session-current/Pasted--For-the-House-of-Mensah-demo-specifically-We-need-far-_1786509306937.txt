9. Ground / Grass / Tree Bark / Indoor Walls
These four folders account for most of the ZIP's size.
Together they are roughly:

Grass          ~502 MB
Ground         ~433 MB
Tree Bark      ~289 MB
Indoor Walls   ~144 MB

So DO NOT put these entire folders in Git.
These are largely material/source libraries.
They contain:

diffuse
normal
height
AO
metallic
smoothness
edge

These are useful when you're building a 3D renderer.
But the Niakofa RPG shouldn't carry hundreds of megabytes of unused material maps.
For production:
Keep only the textures actually referenced by the game.
For example:

niakofa-legacy/assets/world/materials/
    village-ground.png
    cocoa-soil.png
    coastal-sand.png
    red-earth.png
    forest-floor.png
    family-compound-ground.png
