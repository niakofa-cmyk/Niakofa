
Where the vision isn't fully backed by code yet
"Livable wage" is the biggest gap. The $5-per-task guaranteed minimum floor exists to protect against pool depletion — it is not a wage model. There's no per-hour floor, no tenure-based earnings boost, and no dashboard showing a helper "you earned $X today just for helping," which is the emotional core of your pitch. The roadmap has a fully-specced tenure-tier design (Neighbor → Pillar → Anchor → Elder, scaling the floor by helper tenure) ready to implement but not yet shipped — see ROADMAP.md, section 2.
Pay-it-forward money doesn't fully become real, withdrawable money. Helper earnings land in a benevolence_wallet integer column in Postgres. I searched the entire codebase for a stripe.transfers.create() call tied to pool-fronted or pay-it-forward earnings and found zero — every real bank transfer (transfers.create) is hard-gated to payment_type === "immediate" only. So "pay now" helpers can cash out to a bank account; pay-it-forward and pool-fronted helpers accumulate a wallet balance with no automatic payout path yet.
"Legal task" scope is narrower than stated. The category taxonomy was recently expanded (legal_aid, financial_coaching, job_assistance, language_help, mental_health_peer, technology_help were all added — a real, shipped improvement), but there's no legal-task-specific liability/licensing logic, and background checks (background_check_status) are a manual/stub field, not wired to an actual Checkr-style verification provider — worth prioritizing given childcare/senior_care are live categories.
