## 2. requests.ts — livable-wage floor now reaches immediate-pay tasks
`requests.ts` → replace `artifacts/api-server/src/routes/requests.ts`.

**What changed:** the guaranteed-minimum wage floor (the $15/hr-scaled
"livable wage" mechanism) previously only applied to pay-it-forward and
goodwill tasks — immediate-pay tasks were explicitly skipped
(`payment_type !== "immediate"`). That meant a requester paying immediately
could set any price, including well under the floor, with zero enforcement.
This was one of the two concrete gaps flagged in the vision audit.

Now every completed task — immediate, pay-it-forward, or goodwill — gets
topped up by the Community Pool to the floor if what the helper's actually
getting falls short. For immediate tasks, the requester's own Stripe payment
still counts toward the floor first (no double-pay); the pool only covers
the gap. The top-up lands in the helper's Goodwill Fund wallet, same as it
already does for underfunded pledges — separate from, and additive to, the
Stripe Connect payout.

This does NOT add category-based pricing (moving labor vs. tutoring, etc.)
— that still needs the pricing decision from you I described earlier. This
just makes the existing flat/hours-scaled floor apply everywhere instead of
skipping one payment type entirely.
