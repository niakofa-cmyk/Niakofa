The one real bug: BestMatchCard gets buried by the expanded sheet
BestMatchCard and BottomSheet used to be either/or; now they render together (map.tsx ~1239–1260), which was the right call. But they're both z-20 siblings, with BestMatchCard mounted before BottomSheet in the JSX. At equal z-index, later DOM siblings paint on top — so the moment a helper drags the sheet open (h-[55vh], sheet top well above BestMatchCard's bottom-44), the sheet's opaque card fully covers the Best Match prompt. It's still in the DOM and still clickable, just invisible underneath the sheet, which is worse than not being there — taps land on sheet content instead.
Fix is small: either bump BestMatchCard to z-30 (matching the Filters/Layers/Recenter row logic you already used for reachability), or — probably nicer — dock it as a pinned header inside the collapsed sheet's peek area so it never gets covered no matter the sheet's state. I'd lean toward the z-index bump first since it's a one-line fix; the docking approach is a bigger refactor if you want it later.
