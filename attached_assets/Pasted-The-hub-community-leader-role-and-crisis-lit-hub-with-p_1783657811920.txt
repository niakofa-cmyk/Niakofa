The one real problem: pledges aren't backed by money or authority
I traced POST /griot/hubs/:id/pledges end to end. Right now:
* It takes a from_hub_id, an amount, inserts a row, and returns success — there's no Stripe charge, no card capture, and no link to community_pool_ledger anywhere in the route.
* There's no check that the pledging user actually leads or belongs to from_hub_id. Any authenticated user can submit "Fort Worth pledges $10,000 to Accra" attributing it to a hub they have no relationship to.
* The frontend modal then tells the user "Pledge sent 🙏 ... has been recorded" — language that reads as confirmation something real happened.
Put together, this is currently a system where anyone can publicly claim, in a crisis, that a large sum is coming from a named community — with no money moving and no way to verify the claim came from someone authorized to speak for that hub. In a real crisis, that's not a minor rough edge; it's the exact shape of a rumor that could make people believe help is coming when it isn't. This needs one of two fixes before it's exposed to real users:
1. Wire it to real payment (my original recommendation) — pledge triggers an actual Stripe charge, and on success writes a sponsor_contribution row into community_pool_ledger scoped to the receiving hub, same as the rest of your pool machinery. Then status: 'pledged' only ever becomes 'fulfilled' when a payment actually clears, not on insert.

No audit trail on crisis clear (who cleared it, why) — worth a one-line crisis_resolved_note column, since this is the one action that makes a real emergency disappear from the map.
PledgeSchema caps a single pledge at $100,000 with no per-user daily/velocity limit — worth capping lower or rate-limiting per-user until real payment is wired, since right now it's free to submit.

One real gap in the new feature, worth fixing before you rely on it: cross-hub pledges are ledger-only right now — a diaspora_hub_pledges row with status: "pledged" gets created, but nothing charges a card, moves pool money, or ever flips the status to "fulfilled". It's a promise, not a payment. Given your vision explicitly says "helpers get paid," I'd treat this as the top priority to close: either wire pledged_by's pledge into a Stripe PaymentIntent the way /pool/contribute already does, or clearly label it in the UI as a public commitment (like a crowdfunding pledge) rather than an instant transfer, so users aren't misled about when money actually moves.
