HIGH
BUG-5-H01 — artifacts/api-server/src/middlewares/auth.ts: JWT exp claim is verified but iat (issued-at) is not validated against a token blacklist or minimum-issue-time; revoked tokens remain valid until expiry.
BUG-5-H02 — artifacts/api-server/src/routes/users.ts: Profile update endpoint accepts role field in the request body and passes it through to the DB update without stripping it; any authenticated user can self-elevate to admin.
