I would not make the Sankofa Bird a game character in the way Pokémon GO works. I would make it feel like a living navigation companion.
That's a subtle but powerful difference.

Why it feels like Pokémon GO
Pokémon GO made the player feel alive because things weren't static.
The avatar
* rotates
* walks
* pauses
* turns
* follows GPS
The world feels connected to your movement.
Niakofa can achieve that same feeling.
Not because it's a game...
but because the bird is always reacting.

The Egg never moves
This is my favorite detail.
Everything moves...
except the egg.
The bird protects it.
Imagine this:
The bird banks left.
The body tilts.
The wings flap.
The tail swings.
But the beak subtly compensates.
The egg stays level.
That tiny animation reinforces the Sankofa philosophy:
Even while moving forward, it carefully carries wisdom from the past.
Players may never consciously notice it, but they'll feel the intention behind the design.

Your Flutter code doesn't animate every feather manually. It simply updates inputs such as:

bird.heading = compassHeading;
bird.speed = gpsSpeed;
bird.isNavigating = true;
bird.isHelping = true;
bird.notification = true;

The Rive state machine smoothly blends between animations based on those values.
