24. Navigation Intelligence
Rather than simply pointing north, the bird should anticipate movement:
* It gradually turns toward the next waypoint instead of snapping instantly.
* Before a left or right turn, it subtly looks in that direction and begins banking, making the motion feel predictive.
* As the user approaches a destination, wingbeats slow and the bird transitions into a gentle hover before landing.


Built with a improved and enhanced state-machine animation system that can transition fluidly between dozens of natural behaviors while remaining lightweight enough to run smoothly on iOS, Android, and the web.
