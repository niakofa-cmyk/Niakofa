Enhancements & Strategic Recommendations
1.AI-Powered Dispatch: Enhance the dispatch worker (dispatch-worker.ts) with machine learning algorithms that consider helper skills, historical reliability, real-time traffic, and current workload to optimize request assignments.
2.Advanced Trust & Safety: Implement automated anomaly detection to flag suspicious patterns (e.g., rapid request cancellations, frequent low ratings) for immediate admin review, augmenting the manual reporting system.
3.Comprehensive Observability: Integrate Application Performance Monitoring (APM) tools (e.g., Sentry, Datadog) to track frontend crashes, API latency, and WebSocket connection health in real-time.
4.Multi-Modal Notifications: Expand the push notification system (push.ts) to include SMS and email fallbacks for critical alerts (e.g., SOS or Emergency requests) to ensure delivery when users are offline or push services fail.
5.Civic Integration Portals: Develop dedicated portals for local community organizations (e.g., food banks, shelters) to manage their profiles, post bulk requests, and verify volunteers directly within the Niakofa platform.

1. AI traffic is different
Your normal app traffic:
* profiles
* posts
* messages
* orders
* locations
Your AI traffic:
* conversations
* searches
* recommendations
* reasoning
They scale differently.
A huge AI conversation spike should not slow down the main app.
