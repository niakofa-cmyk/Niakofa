The gap that opened up by fixing the other one
This is the thing worth your attention now: childcare, senior_care, and medical are live in the category picker with zero extra safeguards. I checked — no background-check requirement, no minimum trust tier, no admin approval gate, nothing that distinguishes claiming a childcare request from claiming a grocery-run request. Any member-tier user (the lowest tier, no verification beyond signup) can claim a request to watch someone's kids or check on an elderly parent, same as picking up milk.
This is the one place I'd push back before it ships, not just note for later: broadening the task taxonomy is exactly right for your vision, but categories involving vulnerable people (kids, elderly, medical) carry liability and trust exposure that groceries/errands don't. Concretely, I'd want to see, before these three categories go live:

A minimum trust tier to claim them (e.g. verified or trusted, not member)
Some background-check or ID-verification flag gating them specifically — you already have a verification.ts route and trust tiers; this would be extending that system, not building new
Possibly a different liability/consent flow for the requester (e.g. an explicit acknowledgment that Niakofa isn't a licensed childcare or homecare provider).
