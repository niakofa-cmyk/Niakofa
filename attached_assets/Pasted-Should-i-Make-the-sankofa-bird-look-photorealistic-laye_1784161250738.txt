Should i Make the sankofa bird look photorealistic  - layed fathers, iridesent highlighs, and a rive stat machine?

Yes—but with one important adjustment.

I would make the Sankofa Bird artistically photorealistic, not hyper-photorealistic. There's a subtle but important difference.

A tiny, fully photorealistic bird on a moving map can become visually noisy and hard to read. The best mascots have a recognizable silhouette first, then realistic details when you zoom in.

I would improve the bird even further
1. Better anatomy

Your current artwork is already beautiful, but I'd refine it for animation.

Slightly longer wings
More flexible tail
Lighter body
Stronger shoulder joints
Better feather overlap

The bird should look capable of sustained flight.

2. More expressive eyes

The eyes are where personality lives.

Add:

blinking,
tiny eye movements,
subtle pupil changes,
catchlights that shift with the lighting.

The user should feel the bird is aware of them.

3. Stabilize the egg

This is something I wouldn't compromise on.

Everything moves...

except the egg.

As the bird banks or accelerates, its neck and head subtly compensate to keep the egg nearly level.

That animation quietly reinforces the Sankofa meaning of carrying wisdom forward with care.

4. Smarter flight

Don't make the bird simply rotate.

Real birds:

look before they turn,
bank into corners,
glide whenever possible,
flap only when needed,
slow down before landing.

Those behaviors will make the mascot feel intelligent.

5. Dynamic feathers

Feathers should respond to movement.

Walking:

gentle flutter.

Running:

stronger wing beats.

Driving:

mostly gliding.

Stopping:

feathers settle naturally.

These secondary motions add realism without overwhelming the user.

6. Lighting

Instead of baking lighting into the artwork, let it react subtly.

The bird should have:

soft ambient shading,
iridescent edge highlights,
a faint glow around the egg,
a soft shadow beneath it.

This creates depth without distracting from the map.

One thing I would not do

I would not use an 8K raster image as the live map marker.

Instead, I'd use the 8K artwork as the master design reference and recreate it as a layered vector character. That gives you:

crisp rendering on any screen size,
smooth animation,
lower battery usage,
scalable assets,
and the flexibility to add lifelike behaviors.
