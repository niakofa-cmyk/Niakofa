One real bug I found (worth fixing)
In requests.ts, the Pay-It-Forward posting limit has a hard block for "defaulted" pledges — the code comment says pledge_status gets set to defaulted automatically "after 90 days with zero repayment" by a "pledge-default worker." That worker doesn't exist. I read pledge-worker.ts in full — it only logs an admin warning after 30 days and sends gentle reminders; nothing ever sets pledge_status = 'defaulted' automatically. Right now the only way a pledge becomes "defaulted" is an admin manually doing it via PATCH /admin/requests/:id/pledge-status. So the documented safety net (people who never repay eventually get capped from posting more Pay-It-Forward requests) doesn't actually trigger on its own — it's a real gap between the code's own comments and its behavior, and it matters for platform sustainability since it's the mechanism that protects your pool from serial non-payers.
What I'd genuinely still recommend adding

Build the missing 90-day auto-default worker (cron/BullMQ job scanning completed_at > 90 days AND pledge_paid < pledge_amount AND pledge_status = 'active', respecting the hardship exclusion) — this is the single highest-leverage fix since it's referenced but not real.
Two-way accountability for helpers, not just requesters — I saw trust scoring and reports, but you may want an explicit "helper reliability" signal (no-shows, late arrivals) feeding into matching priority, since a livable-wage promise only works if helpers are dependable too.
A visible "runway" metric for requesters and the public — how many days can the pool sustain guaranteed minimums at current burn rate — to build trust in the county/sponsor pitch and make the "Pay It Forward" ask feel transparent rather than a black box.
Dispute/mediation flow for partial or disputed completions — I saw reports and moderation but didn't see a structured "helper says done, requester disagrees" resolution path; worth confirming that exists before you scale volume.
A public/marketing-facing dashboard for county sponsors showing impact per dollar (tasks completed, families helped) — you have the ledger data already (community-pool.ts, gov-sponsors.ts); this is mostly a reporting layer on top of what's there.
