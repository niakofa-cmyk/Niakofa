---
name: Moment retention and responses
description: Product and privacy boundaries for Community Moment archives, featuring, and video responses.
---

Opt-in Moment archives are private to the creator and remain available until the creator permanently deletes them. Only approved, published Moments can be archived; do not retain a pending moderation item through the archive path when the creator cannot manage it there. Featuring is a separate creator action, only for a Moment in the creator's current Community, and must not broaden its audience. Attributed video responses require the source creator's consent, a ready source video, a ready response video, and same-Community access.

**Why:** The creator explicitly chose retention until deletion, while audience scoping and consent are essential privacy boundaries. Pending items are not exposed in the creator archive and otherwise could become unmanageable retained media.

**How to apply:** Preserve these conditions across API authorization, expiry cleanup, media access, creator collection controls, and future Moments features.
