name: Community-service vision
description: Product direction for neighbor help, flexible Pay It Forward contributions, and fair helper compensation.
---

The user’s product vision is a community-service platform where neighbors help one another; requesters may pay now, contribute later in flexible amounts and time frames, or seek volunteer help; helpers should benefit from fair, sustainable compensation. The detailed current-state review and recommended implementation sequence are in `docs/COMMUNITY_SERVICE_VISION_REFERENCE.md`.

Pay It Forward is optional for requesters: no positive pledge amount means no pledge. Once a positive pledge exists, retain the existing two-year overdue and trust-score lifecycle; repayments are manually initiated, not automatic card charges.

**Why:** the user asked to preserve the product vision and make Pay It Forward optional without changing its lifecycle.

**How to apply:** keep request forms and defaults opt-in; do not weaken the overdue/default/trust behavior for existing pledges. Distinguish product vision from current functionality, and do not claim a pledge is debt-free or helper pay is a guaranteed livable wage unless policy and funding support that claim.
