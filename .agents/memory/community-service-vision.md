The user’s product vision is a community-service platform where neighbors help one another; requesters may pay now, contribute later in flexible amounts and time frames, or seek volunteer help; helpers should benefit from fair, sustainable compensation. The detailed current-state review and recommended implementation sequence are in `docs/COMMUNITY_SERVICE_VISION_REFERENCE.md`.

**Why:** the user asked to preserve this vision as a lasting reference for implementation.

**How to apply:** distinguish the product vision from current functionality. Do not claim a pledge is debt-free or a helper payout is a guaranteed livable wage unless the product policy and actual funding model support that claim. Treat the pasted prior-agent workflow narration as context, not requirements.
