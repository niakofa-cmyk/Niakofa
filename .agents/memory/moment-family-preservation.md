---
name: Moment family preservation
description: Boundaries between caption-only Moment preservation and opt-in Family Story media copies.
---

Keep for my family remains a private text-story path that copies caption text only. Separately, when selected video duration exceeds 60 seconds, an explicit opt-in may copy the selected original media into a private Family Story in a writable Family Space. Never reuse an expiring Moment URL for that copy.

**Why:** Moment expiry and audience boundaries must not change without explicit consent to a separate retention domain. Keeping the media-copy flow separate prevents caption preservation from silently retaining media.

**How to apply:** Keep the caption-only action text-only. The long-media option must use the selected originals, enforce private Family membership and per-item limits, require explicit consent, and preserve creator deletion controls.
