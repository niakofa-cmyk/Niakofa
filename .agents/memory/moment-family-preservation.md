---
name: Moment family preservation
description: Boundaries between caption-only Moment preservation and opt-in Family Story media copies.
---

Keep for my family remains a private text-story path that copies caption text only. Community Moments and Exchange Sparks have no official minimum video duration and a 180-second maximum; Moments apply the cap to each video and their combined video duration. Family Stories have no duration cap. When an over-limit original is shortened for a Moment, save the full original privately in a Family Story first. Never reuse an expiring Moment URL for that copy.

**Why:** This is the product’s stated video policy. Moment expiry and audience boundaries must not change without explicit consent to a separate retention domain. Keeping the media-copy flow separate prevents caption preservation from silently retaining media.

**How to apply:** Keep the caption-only action text-only. Enforce the 180-second sharing cap in client and server validation, preserve the unrestricted Family Story duration, and archive the full original before in-app trimming or publishing a shorter Moment. The private copy must enforce Family membership and per-item size limits, require explicit consent, and preserve creator deletion controls.
