---
name: Moment family preservation
description: Why keeping a Moment in Family Vault copies only its caption.
---

Keep for my family is a private text-story preservation path, not a way to make a Moment's expiring photo, video, or audio permanent.

**Why:** Copying media into another retention domain would silently bypass Moment expiry and imply consent to a different audience and storage lifetime.

**How to apply:** Preserve caption text only under the creator's authority. Any future media-preservation feature needs an explicit audience, consent, storage, and deletion policy rather than reusing this path.
