---
name: Exchange history retention
description: Private history removal is distinct from exact fixture cleanup or deletion of shared safety records.
---

Private removal of finished Exchange history must not erase another participant's
record, disputes, moderation evidence, or attached media. Label it “Remove from
my history,” not permanent deletion, and disclose retention before confirmation.

**Why:** Listing and pickup records are shared, and existing safety references
restrict deletion. Clearing one person's view must not remove evidence belonging
to someone else or imply that storage objects were deleted.

**How to apply:** Keep active coordination visible, enforce participant ownership
on the server, and treat private history removal as insufficient for an exact
production-fixture deletion gate. Obtain explicit permission to retain disposable
history or use an approved exact-record cleanup procedure before that acceptance
scenario creates production records.
