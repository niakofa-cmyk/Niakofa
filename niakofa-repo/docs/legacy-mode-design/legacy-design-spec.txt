The Living Family Legacy Experience
The gameplay becomes the motivation to preserve culture.
The preservation becomes the content.
The family becomes the world.
The stories become the quests.
The ancestors become the heroes.

[Full spec content: see attached_assets/Pasted--The-Living-Family-Legacy-Experience-The-gameplay-becom_1785441473335.txt]

Key points:
- Every story added becomes playable
- Every new family member changes the game
- Every interview unlocks quests
- Every migration changes the map
- Every photograph creates locations
- Every birthday unlocks memories
- Every grave location becomes somewhere to visit
- Every family achievement becomes something to discover

5 Development Phases:
Phase 1 – Foundation: Family Tree, Family Vault, Oral Story Recording, Cards, Timeline
Phase 2 – Story Intelligence: AI story extraction, historical timeline, quest generation
Phase 3 – Personal RPG: Random ancestor selection, life chapters, historical context
Phase 4 – World Exploration: Interactive maps, family landmarks, GPS discoveries
Phase 5 – Living Family Universe: Cooperative missions, AI Game Master
