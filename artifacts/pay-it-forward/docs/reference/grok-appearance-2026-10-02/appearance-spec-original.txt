Have we established settings, Added and verified the active switch from Dark mode to light mode? Deep Navy (#08182B) and Electric Cyan (#00CFFF) form a cool, high-contrast, modern foundation. To build a complete UI system for a social app, you need warm accent colors to pop against the cool tones, as well as background/surface neutrals that ensure high contrast and WCAG accessibility.
Primary Anchor Colors

Deep Navy (#08182B): Structural base. Best for dark-mode backgrounds, sidebars, top headers, and primary text on light themes.
Electric Cyan (#00CFFF): Brand identifier & interactive accent. Best for primary Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons, active tab indicators, unread badges, and verified checkmarks.
Vibrant Warm Accents
Cool palettes can feel distant without a warm accent to guide the user's eye toward conversion points and notifications.
Coral Sunset (#FF5A5F):
Role: Secondary CTA, notifications, "Like" hearts, alerts, or urgent actions.
Why it works: Sits directly opposite cyan on the color wheel. It provides immediate contrast against both dark navy and cyan.
Electric Amber (#FFB703):
Role: Warnings, ratings, badges, "Add to Village" highlights, or secondary action buttons.
Why it works: Adds warmth and optimism while remaining readable on dark backgrounds.
Supporting Mid-Tones & Neutrals
Soft Ice Blue (#E0F7FC):
Role: Card backgrounds in light mode, active state tints, or subtle hover effects.
Why it works: A 5% tint of Electric Cyan that binds the interface together without overwhelming the screen.
Slate Muted Blue (#4A627A):
Role: Secondary text (timestamps, subheadings, muted icons), borders, and disabled button states.
Why it works: Bridges the gap between deep navy and bright cyan, ensuring secondary elements don't compete with main text.
Off-White Surface (#F8FAFC):
Role: Primary background for Light Mode.
Why it works: Pure #FFFFFF can produce excessive harsh contrast against #08182B; off-white softens readability for long scrolling sessions.

Design & Contrast Notes

Button Text Accessibility: Avoid placing white text directly inside an Electric Cyan (#00CFFF) button. White on #00CFFF fails WCAG AAA contrast standards. Use your Deep Navy (#08182B) for text inside Cyan buttons for high readability.
Gradient Usage: A smooth linear gradient from Deep Navy (#08182B) to a slightly brighter slate/indigo creates a polished backdrop for profile headers or splash screens.
